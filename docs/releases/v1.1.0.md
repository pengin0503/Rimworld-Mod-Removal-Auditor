Read-only, on-demand audit for RimWorld 1.7: choose enabled mods and see which of their content is still present in the loaded save before you disable them. Nothing is changed, deleted or saved. There are no Harmony patches and no tick work.

**A result with no detections is not a guarantee that a mod is safe to remove.** Back up your save and test-load it after disabling a mod.

## Install

Extract `ModRemovalAuditor-1.1.0.zip` into your RimWorld `Mods` folder and enable **Mod Removal Auditor**. Open it with the magnifier icon on the bottom bar, or from *Options → Mod settings → Mod Removal Auditor*. It supports RimWorld 1.7, in English and Japanese.

## What it checks

- **Since 1.0:**
  - all loaded maps: spawned things and everything nested in containers, inventories, apparel, equipment and corpses;
  - caravans, settlement trade stock, transport pods and gravships in flight;
  - world pawns and their gear;
  - terrain on all four layers;
  - things made of a mod's materials.
- **New in 1.1:**
  - pawn data: hediffs, genes, xenotype, traits, abilities, pawn kind and backstories;
  - bills, including surgery bills;
  - research: saved progress, techprints, anomaly knowledge and the current project;
  - quests (root script), factions, ideoligions (memes, precepts and culture), world objects and site parts.

Scopes that are not checked are always listed in the window: thoughts and relations, filters and policies, quest internals, tales and art, mod-specific saved data, and unloaded data.

## Changes since 1.0

- Locations that stand for several instances now say "(+N more)".
- Saved research progress is read without the game accessors that would insert entries into the save.
- The window no longer re-lays out every frame while it is being resized.
- The unused Waylith reference was removed from the build.

## 導入

`ModRemovalAuditor-1.1.0.zip` をRimWorldの `Mods` フォルダーに展開し、**Mod Removal Auditor** を有効にします。画面下部の虫眼鏡アイコン、または「オプション→MODの設定→Mod Removal Auditor」から開けます。対応バージョンはRimWorld 1.7、言語は日本語と英語です。

## 調査範囲

- **1.0から：**
  - 読み込み済みの全マップの配置物と、容器・インベントリ・衣服・装備・死体の中身；
  - キャラバン、拠点の交易在庫、輸送ポッド、飛行中のグラビシップ；
  - ワールドポーンとその所持品；
  - 床・地形の4レイヤー；
  - MODの素材で作られた物。
- **1.1で追加：**
  - ポーンのデータ：健康状態、遺伝子、ゼノタイプ、特性、能力、ポーンの種類、経歴；
  - 作業指示（手術の予定を含む）；
  - 研究：保存された進捗、技術設計図、アノマリーの知識、現在の研究；
  - クエスト（元になるスクリプト）、勢力、イデオロギー（ミーム・戒律・文化）、ワールド上の物とサイトの構成要素。

調べない範囲は常にウィンドウに表示します：思考・人間関係、フィルターと方針、クエスト内部、物語と美術品、MOD独自の保存データ、読み込まれていないデータ。

## 1.0からの変更点
